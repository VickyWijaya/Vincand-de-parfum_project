## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimacy, craftsmanship, and architectural restraint of a Parisian haute parfumerie. Designed for discerning collectors and seekers of sensory luxury, the interface mirrors a physical art book or gallery catalog rather than an ephemeral digital storefront. The emotional response is one of reverence, calm, and contemplative desire.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Classicalism**:
- **Atmospheric Grandeur:** Monumental scale contrast between majestic, high-contrast serif headlines and quiet, tracked-out utilitarian metadata.
- **Museum-Grade Layouts:** Asymmetrical compositions, expansive breathing room, quiet pacing, and deliberate negative space emulate archival gallery walls and high-fashion printed editions.
- **Sensory Restraint:** Interactivity is subtle and dignified—relying on whisper-quiet opacity shifts, soft hair-thin boundaries, and measured transitions rather than hyperactive micro-interactions.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in sensory tactile materials: aged parchment, dark flacon glass, cast bronze, and warm alabaster.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (Neutral Light):** `#F9F6F0` (Warm Ivory) serves as the default page base, with `#F5F1EA` (Soft Cream) and `#E6DFD5` (Soft Taupe) providing architectural field stratification without harsh cuts.
- **Deep Contrast Enclosures:** `#121212` (Abyssal Charcoal) and `#26211E` (Warm Espresso) frame intimate nightfall movements, extract concentrations, and campaign focal points.
- **Typographic Core:** `#111111` and `#1A1A1A` for display titles, lending archival weight. Secondary editorial prose utilizes `#4A4541`, while micro-captions and metadata settle into `#7A7571` (Muted Stone).
- **Accents & Finishes:** `#C5A880` (Muted Champagne Gold) and `#8C6D46` (Subtle Bronze) are used with strict conservation—reserved for olfactive pyramid highlights, seals, formulation grades, and focused states.
- **Structural Dividing Lines:** Border rules are never structural dividers; they are hairline fractures in the surface rendered with `#E2DCD2` in light containers and `rgba(245, 241, 234, 0.12)` against deep charcoal.

## Typography

The typography stages a dialogue between romantic nineteenth-century French publishing and twentieth-century modernist cataloging.

### Structural Roles
- **Display & Titles (Bodoni Moda):** Imparts aristocratic elegance, vertical drama, and editorial gravitas. Large sizes run with tight, precise tracking to heighten letterform contrast, while italic cuts are deployed sparingly for scent families, olfactive movements, or poetic dedications.
- **Narrative & Storytelling Body (Inter & Bodoni Moda):** Curatorial statements and perfumer stories employ Bodoni Moda in relaxed leading (`body-lead`). Standard compositions, ingredient profiles, and practical instructions transition to Inter light (`font-weight: 300`) with generous leading for supreme digital clarity.
- **Catalog Archetypes & Micro-Labels:** Formulations, concentrations, batch numbers, volume indicators, and interactive anchors leverage uppercase, tracked-out Inter (`0.22em` to `0.26em`). This creates a stark, hyper-modern stamp reminiscent of clean clinical apothecary marks and high-fashion tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy rejects dense commercial grids in favor of **asymmetric editorial pacing** and **museum-grade breathing room**. The screen is treated as an unbound folio where imagery and text balance through intentional tension.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column asymmetric grid within a max canvas of `1440px`. Gutters sit at `3rem` (`48px`) with page edge offsets ranging from `4rem` to `6rem`. Columns are paired into unequal fields (e.g., a 7-column media span anchored beside a 4-column offset typography cluster, with a 1-column negative rift).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` gutters and margins, converting tight overlaps into staggered linear sequences while sustaining editorial white space.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters. Staggered asymmetrical shifts collapse into single-column vertical narratives, using expansive vertical margins (`space-xl`) between bottle stills, notes, and calls to inquiry.

### Composition Principles
- **Asymmetrical Offsets:** Text containers frequently shift 10%–15% below visual baselines, evoking monograph book layouts.
- **Immersion Panels:** Alternations between wide Ivory sections and deep Espresso full-bleed panels pace the reader’s journey from day to night formulations.
- **Negative Space Conservation:** Minimum vertical spacing between major campaign sections is `space-2xl` (`6rem`) up to `space-3xl` (`9rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic drop shadows, floating cards, and standard UI depth cues. Visual elevation is entirely tactile and atmospheric, constructed using **tonal layering**, **hairline separation**, and **soft photographic contrast**.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base: Primary Ivory (`#F9F6F0`) or Charcoal (`#121212`).
  - Secondary Tier: Subtle surface shift via Soft Cream (`#F5F1EA`) or Warm Espresso (`#26211E`) insets.
  - Overlay Sheets / Drawers: Deep translucent scrims (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.45)` light; `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)` dark) backed by a gentle `backdrop-blur-sm` (4px to 8px) simulating heavy frosted flacon glass.
- **Outlines & Hairlines:**
  - Edges and partitions are rendered exclusively through 1px fine hairline borders (`#E2DCD2` on ivory, `rgba(245, 241, 234, 0.10)` on espresso/charcoal). No thick strokes or pillowed bevels.
- **Ambient Presence:**
  - Floating items (such as the bespoke cart drawer, scent discovery modal, or sample tray) cast only an ultra-subtle, deeply diffuse natural shadow: `box-shadow: 0 24px 60px -12px rgba(26, 22, 19, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **sharp, pristine geometry (`roundedness: 0`)**. 

- **Architectural Edges:** All interactive elements, image containers, input fields, badges, and modals possess pure 90-degree corners (`0px` border radius). This deliberate severity echoes bespoke packaging boxes, heavy crystal perfume caps, and letterpress cards.
- **Imagery Containers:** Visuals are framed as razor-cut vignettes or edge-to-edge museum installations. No rounded corners or pill badges are permitted anywhere across the interface.
- **Structural Alignment:** Linear discipline is paramount—every visual element snaps directly to sharp hairline anchors and strict axes.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Editorial Button:** Sharp rectangular silhouette with a 1px border. Dark variants use an ivory ground (`#F5F1EA`) with deep charcoal typography (`#121212`); light variants use an espresso ground (`#1A1A1A`) with warm ivory typography. Micro-label typography (`label-caps-spaced`), padding `1.125rem 2.5rem`. Hover states initiate an effortless color inversion or gentle hairline tone shift over 400ms ease.
- **Ghost Action:** No background fill, 1px border in `#C5A880` or `#1A1A1A`, matching uppercase micro-text. Hover reveals a quiet 10% tinted background wash.
- **Text Link / Monograph Anchor:** Raw uppercase text with a discreet hair-thin underline offset by 6px. On hover, the underline expands smoothly from center or transitions from stone to champagne gold.

### Olfactory Olfactive Pyramids & Accord Chips
- **Chips & Tags:** Sharp, unrounded, wafer-thin boxes. Height `28px`, padding `0 12px`. Light cream background (`#F5F1EA`) with stone text (`#7A7571`) and a 1px hairline border (`#E2DCD2`).
- **Olfactive Accord Pyramid:** Segmented three-tier table (Top, Heart, Base) framed by hair-thin horizontal stone lines. Each tier opens with a tracked uppercase label (`TÊTE`, `CŒUR`, `FOND`), followed by a curated serif listing of pure extracts.

### Fragrance & Product Cards
- **Composition:** Asymmetrical layout featuring a generous aspect ratio (3:4 or 4:5) image container framed by a 1px translucent border or raw ivory ground.
- **Labeling Hierarchy:**
  1. Micro-label concentration badge (e.g., `EXTRAIT DE PARFUM — 100ML`) set in `label-caps-subtle`.
  2. Fragrance name in `headline-md` Bodoni Moda.
  3. Key olfactory notes summarized in light stone Inter italic.
  4. Subtle price marker placed opposite the title or tucked under the primary note line.
- **Hover State:** Image initiates a calm, imperceptible zoom (`scale(1.025)`) accompanied by a secondary imagery fade-in showcasing bottle extraction or campaign art over 600ms cubic-bezier.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Underlined Apothecary Fields:** Inputs eschew full box boundaries, relying instead on a crisp bottom hairline (`1px solid #E2DCD2`).
- **Text & Float Behavior:** Placeholder text in Inter `300`, which transforms into a floating `label-caps-subtle` above the field upon engagement. Focused states transition the bottom hairline from neutral to `#C5A880` (Champagne Gold).
- **Dark Mode Adjustment:** Underline sits at `rgba(245, 241, 234, 0.15)` with off-white value entry.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Volume & Scent Discovery Selectors:** Designed as architectural square tiles. The selected state applies a 1px champagne gold frame with a delicate interior dot or filled border accent, displaying volume (`50 ML`, `100 ML`) in tracked sans-serif with price delta markers.

### Lists & Archival Tables
- **Edition & Formulation Indices:** Full-width rows isolated by 1px top and bottom hair-thin rules. Alternating columns catalog vintage, batch, noses, and master notes. Cursor row-hover shifts background subtly from `#F9F6F0` to `#F5F1EA` without disturbing layout geometry.
